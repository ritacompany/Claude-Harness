# Inbox

Items captured but not yet researched. Each line keeps where it came from. When an item becomes an
entry, its line moves to INDEX.md and is deleted here. An item seen in two lists is kept once and
names the other list.

Kind is a first guess, fixed at research: rule (a default that applies whenever its condition
holds), technique (an opt in move), term (vocabulary the engine should use precisely), texture (a
surface or background treatment), trend (a look tied to its moment; record when it reads dated).
