# Design canvases and comment boards

A board he opens to explore, comment on or edit designs: the Claude Code design canvas, pan and
zoom boards, reference boards.

- Design options and explorations live here; his pick goes where he names (Figma or Framer).
  (E1454, E1444, E1462, E1492, E1526)
- He can drag anything freely: groups stay grouped when moved and layout grids never block a drag.
  (E1779, E1775, E1778, E1824)
- Text objects carry cap height or baseline trim and min and max width controls when he is
  composing type. (E1397, E1826)
- Pan, zoom, fit and full screen, with an easy way back out of any zoomed or opened view.
  (E1466, E1580, E1637, E2035)
- Keep a main source and separate branches; a new round never builds on a branch he did not like;
  branches are cut or merged only on his word. (E0018, E1973, E1412, E1413)
- His comments on the board are how he approves, cuts and promotes; read them all before the next
  round. (E1432, E1999, E1975, E2002)
- The canvas tool itself is his to shape: changes he makes to how it works are kept for later
  sessions. (E1777, E1781)
- Read the live board, his edits included, before every publish. (E1834, E2141, E2097)
- Draw on the real design system, with its components mounted, when the canvas shows his product.
  (E0163, E1346, E1391)
