# Explainers, decks, documents and guides

Pages he reads or presents: explainers, crash courses, research pages, feature books, decks,
presenter notes, guides, handoff documents.

## Explainers and research pages

- Visual first: site maps, flow maps and real screens, with about a quarter of the text a document
  would have, plus a short version in chat. (E1440, E1442, E1441, E1607)
- Each item shows its source context beside it, and the source link sits right under the insight
  it supports. (E1540, E0066, E0307, E0842)
- Many categories go in a table or grouped sections, each item named so he can find it again.
  (E0827, E0924, E0925)
- A structural question gets one clear map of the parts and how they relate, not a set of options.
  (E0642, E0683, E0687)
- A feature book is simple: one page per feature that says what it is and shows its main screens,
  each screen big enough to speak to on its own, flipping like a book and usable as slides.
  (E2162, E2163, E2164, E2166, E2170)

## Decks

- Slides are visual aids: minimal text, large type, white space; the audience never reads while he
  talks. Several slides broken up by topic. (E0451, E0538, E0540, E1100)
- Every slide fits the screen with no scrolling and no empty band. (E0697, E0702, E1536)
- Dark background, Heebo and IBM Plex Sans Mono, clean and styled. (E0667, E1501, E1537)
- His real screens and components at a readable size and full resolution. (E0539, E0541, E1106)
- A separate short speaker outline holds the detail, not the slides. (E0669, E0694, E0698)
- Guides and presenter notes are clearly separated sections with bold titles and numbered steps,
  so he knows where he is while using them live. (E0633, E0703, E1503)

## Documents for other people

- Downloadable PDF in the white house style he approved: clear hierarchy, smart spacing, no section
  broken across pages, no half empty pages. Slides as pptx, transcripts as Markdown.
  (E0317, E0318, E0777, E0804, E0700)
- Short and scannable, nothing the reader can see elsewhere. (E0306, E0806, E0840)
- A handoff document stands alone: why, then the solution, then the steps. (E0565, E0566, E0567)
- A developer handoff gives only what Figma cannot show: behaviour, rules, edge cases.
  (E0353, E0421, E0422, E0509)
