# Review and decision boards

Any page where he reviews work, picks between options, answers questions or approves items.

## Layout

- The screen or item under review takes at least three quarters of the page, in a zoom and pan
  view with a margin around it; only the current item shows; previous and next sit together and
  the arrow keys work; nothing sits at the bottom and nothing makes him scroll to answer.
  (E1817, E2017, E2051, E2048, E2050)
- The option switch sits at the top of the box that holds the screen. (E2051, E0303)
- Full screen and zoom are there, and he can always get back out. (E1466, E2035, E1580)
- Dark, black and white, medium weight type, near his product look but not his product.
  (E2019, E2025, E2157, E2022)

## Answering

- He picks per section, not per whole set, and every pick can carry a free text note.
  (E1327, E1328, E1463, E1493)
- Every multiple choice lists the recommended options plus Other with its own text field.
  (E0270, E1493, E2028)
- Each question opens with one plain sentence on what the thing is and which screen it sits on.
  (E2079, E2078, E2082, E1347)
- Settle what the sources or references answer before the board is built and never ask him that;
  put design choices to him as picks. (E0063, E0176, E0260, E2076, E2150, E2015, E2093)
- He can comment on the specific thing he means; each comment shows who wrote it without retyping
  a name; he can give his notes by voice. (E2004, E1581, E1577, E1578, E1735, E2001)
- No tags, markers or annotations drawn on the design; notes sit beside it, short and collapsible.
  (E0962, E0964, E1370)

## Saving and hand back

- Every answer and comment autosaves; leaving and coming back loses nothing.
  (E1576, E1581, E2027, E2031)
- The board shows a done state when everything is answered and names exactly what is still open.
  (E2052, E2053)
- Finish sends everything back once; the session reads the saved answers without him saying he is
  done and without a reply per click. (E1819, E2030, E1813, E2034)

## Boards for other people

- For a developer or stakeholder: screens left to right in flow order, notes where something is
  new or changed and why, deleted screens listed apart, every status covered, a place beside each
  flow for problems raised. (E0637, E0638, E0640, E1030, E1111)
- For the CEO or leadership: extremely organised, real screens exactly as designed, opens with what
  was worked on, positives and negatives for the user, a clear place to approve each item.
  (E0653, E0635, E0529, E0536, E0630)
